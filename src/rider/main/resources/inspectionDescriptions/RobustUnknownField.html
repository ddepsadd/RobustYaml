Reports keys of a Robust Toolbox component or prototype that have no matching data field in C#.

Fields are collected from [DataField], [IdDataField], [ParentDataField], [AbstractDataField] and [IncludeDataField] along the inheritance chain of the class registered for that component or prototype kind. Keys of nested structures are not checked: they belong to their own classes.

The index is a heuristic over C# sources, so a rare miss is possible; the severity is a weak warning for that reason.
